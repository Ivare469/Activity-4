    Evacuation Reminders

    • Keep emergency contacts written down in case mobile networks are weak.
    • Charge power banks and flashlights before the storm approaches.
    • Store clean water, food, and important documents in waterproof containers.
    • Check local barangay notices and follow official instructions when available.
    • Prepare a meeting place for family members in case of flooding or power loss.
